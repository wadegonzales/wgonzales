Visual Identity

Tools Used
01.

Classic Hollywood

Classic Hollywood is a glamorous event theme inspired by the golden era of American cinema, featuring vintage theater marquees, Art Deco elements, warm golden lighting, and luxurious red-carpet aesthetics. The theme evokes sophistication, prestige, and timeless elegance, capturing the charm of Hollywood's most iconic period.

02.

Iridescence

Iridescence is a vibrant gala theme inspired by the shimmering, color-shifting qualities of light. Featuring holographic effects, luminous gradients, and radiant visual elements, it creates a modern and elegant atmosphere that symbolizes creativity, excellence, and the beauty of diversity.
